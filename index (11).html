Box score · Measurement plan

How we'll prove it moved the needle

You said the data story is key. Here's how this session would be measured, from tonight to the end of the season, using Kirkpatrick's four levels.

LvlWhat we measureHowWhen
1ReactionConfidence and session ratingPre/post pulse (you just did it) plus a 1-question ratingDay 0
2LearningCan leads tell open from leading questions and run GROW?Quick check and scenario score, target 80%+Day 0
3BehaviorAre data-backed 1:1s actually happening?1:1s logged per lead, plus a team pulse item: “My lead gives me useful feedback.” Belonging check: did every team member get a 1:1, or only the regulars?Day 30
4ResultsDoes it show up for guests and team members?Section guest satisfaction and seasonal retention, pilot sections vs. similar sectionsSeason end
Honest read

A rise in guest scores suggests a link. It doesn't prove one. Comparing pilot sections with similar sections that haven't started keeps the readout to leadership credible.

The story you'd take to leadership

“Pilot sections' guest satisfaction rose from 82% to 86% while similar sections held at 85%. Recommendation: expand to every Guest Services lead before next season.”
